Studio tab: 3 mockup options

How to compare. Each option shows two screens: 1) Studio home and 2) Job Kit (job details, live previews in every size, caption, download). Flip dark mode with the button, and resize the window: phone stacks everything, ultrawide uses the full width.
Option A is what the build team has started, so it is the baseline. B trades power for guidance (one step at a time). C trades guidance for speed on many jobs. Tell me A, B, C, or a mix (for example A's Job Kit with C's board as the "Many jobs at once" screen).
